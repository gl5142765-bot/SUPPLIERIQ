Day -1 

problem statement
SupplierIQ is an AI-powered supplier risk intelligence platform that predicts whether a supplier is likely to be risky based on structured business data. The goal is to help procurement teams detect potential supplier problems early, reduce manual review, and support faster, more informed sourcing decisions.

Who will use SupplierIQ and why?
SupplierIQ will be used by procurement teams, supplier managers, and risk/compliance stakeholders to identify risky suppliers early and support better sourcing decisions.

Project Scope
SupplierIQ will build a machine learning system that predicts supplier risk from structured business data. The project will include data understanding, cleaning, feature engineering, model training, evaluation, explainability, and deployment through an API and dashboard. It will focus on helping procurement teams identify risky suppliers early so they can take action before business disruption happens.

Executive Summary
SupplierIQ is an AI-powered supplier risk intelligence platform designed to help businesses detect risky suppliers before they cause delays, quality issues, or compliance problems. The system will 
analyse supplier data,
generate a risk prediction,
and provide a clear explanation of the result for business users. 

The final product will include a trained model, a FastAPI backend, a Streamlit dashboard, and deployment-ready code for real-world use.
